About

I lead human-AI interaction research at Suno, where people make music with AI. I joined in March 2024 as one of the first 20 employees, started the UX research team, and run it as an AI-first lab. I work with the founders and product team on what we build next.

Before that I spent three and a half years as a Research Scientist at Spotify, where I worked on the AI DJ and on recommendations for music and podcasts. I have a PhD in Computer Science from Brown, where I built apps that helped thousands of people run experiments on their own sleep.

One question runs through all of it. When AI does the work, how does the result still feel like yours?

My answer is guided agency. People want AI to handle the hard part, and they still want to steer. Most products get one half right. The gap that's left is often small, the last 10% between what the AI made and what the person had in mind, and it's where people decide whether the result is theirs.

It's also why I measure what people notice. A model can improve on every benchmark and still feel the same to the person using it.
