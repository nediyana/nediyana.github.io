About

I lead human-AI interaction research at Suno, where people make music with AI. I joined in March 2024, when we were fewer than 20 people, built the UX research team (five people so far), and work with the founders and product team on what we build next and how we know it's working.

Before that I spent three and a half years as a research scientist at Spotify, working on the AI DJ and on music and podcast recommendations, and did a PhD in computer science at Brown.

The idea behind my work started in that PhD, where I built apps that helped people run experiments on their own sleep. I call it guided agency: the system does the hard part, and people still steer. I wrote more about it in What is guided agency?

I saw it again at Spotify with TastePaths, a map of a music genre. Listeners liked it best when it started from artists they already knew, and they asked to grow and prune the map themselves.
