About

I run UX research at Suno, where people make music with AI. I joined in March 2024, when we were fewer than 20 people, built the UX research team (five people so far), and work with the founders and product team on what we build next.

Before that I spent three and a half years as a research scientist at Spotify, working on the AI DJ and on music and podcast recommendations. I have a PhD in computer science from Brown.

At Spotify we built TastePaths, a map of a music genre centered on artists you already listen to. Listeners found their best music between genres, and they wanted to shape the map themselves. I call this guided agency: the AI does the hard part, and people still steer. I wrote more about it in What is guided agency?

And when it comes to evaluating AI, I trust what people notice more than benchmark scores.
